How to trade

  1. Pick an asset — currencies, crypto, commodities or stocks. Each shows its payout %.
  2. Set the time — how long until your trade expires (5s to 10min).
  3. Set the amount — how much to invest in this trade.
  4. Forecast the price — press Up if you think the price will rise, Down if it will fall.
  5. Collect your payout — if your forecast is right when the trade expires, you get your stake back plus up to 90% profit.
